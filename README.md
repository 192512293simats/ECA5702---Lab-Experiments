# ECA5702 – Nanoelectronics and Devices (Old Lab Manual)

This repository contains experiment materials and simulation records for **ECA5702 – Nanoelectronics and Devices**, based on the **old laboratory manual**.

The repository currently contains nine experiment folders and a copy of the old lab manual. The experiment descriptions below are based only on the folders visible in this GitHub repository; they do not represent every experiment listed in the manual.

For the updated experiment set, refer to the [ECA5702 New Lab Experiments repository](https://github.com/192512293simats/ECA5702-Lab-Experiments-NEW).

## Experiments Available in This Repository

### Experiment 1: Band Structure Simulation for Graphene and CNTs Using NanoHUB
- Studies electronic band structures in nanoscale materials using NanoHUB simulation tools. The old manual's procedure and examples also include nanowire band-structure simulations and comparisons between semiconductor materials.
- **Topics:** band structure, quantum confinement, nanowires, and material-dependent electronic properties.

### Experiment 2: Biosensor and Bio-FET Characteristics Using NanoHUB
- Simulates BioFET sensor behaviour as electrolyte pH changes, including threshold-voltage shifts and transfer characteristics.
- **Topics:** BioFETs, biosensing, pH sensitivity, threshold voltage, and drain-current characteristics.

### Experiment 3: Calculate the Potential of a pH Sensor Using NanoHUB
- Uses the ENBIOS 1D tool described in the old manual to study sensor potential while varying electrolyte pH and insulator dimensions.
- **Topics:** pH sensors, electrolyte–insulator–semiconductor (EIS) structures, sensor potential, and insulator thickness.

### Experiment 4: Demonstration of Quantum Tunneling
- Uses a piece-wise constant potential-barrier simulation to examine particle transmission and reflection when a particle encounters a potential barrier.
- **Topics:** quantum tunnelling, potential-barrier height and width, transmission probability, and reflection coefficients.

### Experiment 5: Kronig–Penney Model
- Simulates the energy-functional-versus-energy response for periodic potential structures. The old manual includes step-well, triangular-well, and sinusoidal-potential examples.
- **Topics:** periodic potentials, allowed energy bands, forbidden bandgaps, and electron behaviour in crystal lattices.

### Experiment 6: MOSFET Scaling Analysis and Subthreshold Swing Calculation Using NanoHUB
- Uses the FetToy simulator to study single-gate MOSFET characteristics by varying gate and drain voltages and observing drain current and transconductance efficiency.
- **Topics:** MOSFET I–V characteristics, threshold voltage, saturation, gate control, and transconductance efficiency.

### Experiment 7: Simulation of Density of States of Quantum-Well Structure with Various Nanometre Thicknesses
- Compares simulation outputs for different nanoscale thicknesses and material configurations, following the procedure recorded in the old manual.
- **Topics:** quantum confinement, energy subbands, and density of states (DOS).
- **Note:** The old manual's experiment title and tool-selection instructions are not fully aligned; check the updated manual for the currently required tool and procedure.

### Experiment 8: Simulation of Energy States of Quantum Dot Using NanoHUB
- Simulates quantum-dot structures and compares their energy states for different dimensions, shapes, materials, and simple or multilayer configurations.
- **Topics:** quantum confinement, discrete energy levels, quantum-dot geometry, and size-dependent electronic properties.

### Experiment 9: Simulation of Tunneling Magnetoresistance (TMR) by Varying Barrier Height Using NanoHUB
- Uses a magnetic tunnel junction simulation to study how changing the barrier height affects tunnelling magnetoresistance.
- **Topics:** magnetic tunnel junctions, spin-dependent tunnelling, barrier properties, and TMR.
